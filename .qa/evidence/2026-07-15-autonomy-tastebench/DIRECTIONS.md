## Selected synthesis

Use A. Borrow C's dedicated `/dogfood` review space, but keep a compact Authority control at
the composer. The control is capability-honest: v1 delegates only validated, versioned
`notebook.update_block`; publishing, sharing, export, deletion, access changes, external sync,
provider/web egress, and file access stay review-required.
