Most AI compliance products still sell another dashboard. The more interesting product sells the completed service.

YC's AI-Native Service Companies request points toward companies that do the work instead of merely helping someone do it.

NodeKit's proposed wedge is an autonomous compliance desk.

User: the compliance lead at a healthcare or enterprise-software company.

Job: deliver an audit-ready customer, regulatory, or security evidence package.

Artifact: a source-linked evidence binder with an explicit exception log.

My take is that the hard part is not generating answers. It is retrieving current evidence, showing provenance, identifying what is missing, routing consequential claims to the right reviewer, and proving what was actually delivered.

This is Blueprint 03/16, not a claim that the service is already autonomous or deployed. The build must prove owner-scoped access, durable state, missing-evidence recovery, approval, and a completed binder.

Which compliance request would be the best real-world stress test for this workflow?
