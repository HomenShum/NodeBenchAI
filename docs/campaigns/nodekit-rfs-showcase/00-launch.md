Most idea showcases stop exactly where the interesting part begins: before anything gets built.

YC published 16 Requests for Startups for Summer 2026. We are turning all 16 into a public NodeKit benchmark.

For each request, NodeKit will have to:

- Choose one user and one urgent job.
- Build one useful vertical slice.
- Deploy and verify the real product path.
- Report the tokens, cost, time, repairs, and human intervention.

My take is that the real test is not whether an AI can produce sixteen attractive concepts. It is whether the same system can convert sixteen very different briefs into products that work, feel right for their domains, and survive independent proof.

Nothing will be called built, autonomous, deployed, or taste-certified until the corresponding receipt supports it. Failed assumptions and unavailable measurements will stay visible.

We are starting with Software for Agents, AI-Native Service Companies, and Dynamic Software Interfaces.

Which of YC's sixteen requests would you pressure-test first?
